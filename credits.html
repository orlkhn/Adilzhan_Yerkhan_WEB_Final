THANK YOU / CREDITS

What we used and why.

Everything that is not our own code or writing is listed here, with its source.

HONEST SOURCES
  • Bootstrap 5.3.6 — grid, form fields, buttons and utility classes (MIT licence). getbootstrap.com. File: css/bootstrap.min.css. It is a framework, not a downloaded page template.
  • jQuery 3.6.1 — fade, slide and animate effects, toasts and accordions (MIT licence). jquery.com. File: js/jquery.min.js.
  • Illustrations — the 16 SVG images in images/ (the night skyline and one stylised drawing for each of the 15 places) were generated for this project as custom SVG code with AI help. They are artistic drawings, not photographs. The small icons are our own inline SVG set (js/icons.js); no emoji are used.
  • AI tools — Claude (Anthropic) and ChatGPT (OpenAI) were used for coding assistance, debugging and design suggestions during development.
  • Content — the 15 places are real venues and landmarks in Astana (Coffee Boom, Okadzaki, Botanical Garden, Presidential Park, Prime Game Hub, Chaplin Cinema, Pingwin Bowling, Triathlon Park, Dodo Pizza, Doner na Abaya, Shaurma Food, Bauyrdaq, Yamato Cyber Club, Baiterek Square, Samal Park). Descriptions were written by the team. Prices are approximate: some come from public pages (sxodim.com, eurasia.travel), the rest are our estimates, and every card says which. They can change, so check with the venue. Cards open Google Maps for the location. There is no backend, booking or payment.
  • Fonts — system fonts only (nothing downloaded).
