 NIGHTSHFT — SOURCES AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENTS
WEB Technologies 1 (Front End) — Final, 2026

PROJECT
NightShift is a student project for choosing and planning evening activities in Astana.
The 15 places are real venues and landmarks in Astana (Coffee Boom, Okadzaki, Botanical Garden, Presidential Park,
Prime Game Hub, Chaplin Cinema, Pingwin Bowling, Triathlon Park, Dodo Pizza, Doner na Abaya, Shaurma Food,
Bauyrdaq, Yamato Cyber Club, Baiterek Square, Samal Park).
Prices are approximate: some come from public pages (sxodim.com, eurasia.travel); the rest are estimates marked on each card.
There is no backend, payment integration, or real booking feature. Saved items exist only in the visitor's browser.
